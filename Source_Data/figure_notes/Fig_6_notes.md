# Fig. 6 Source Data notes

Fig. 6 retains the six primary border-city pairs and the standardized 5-kW city-level economic model used in the original analysis. Detroit and Windsor are excluded from this main figure because their grant-realized audit scenarios are reserved for the Supplementary Information.

Panel a shows central net CAPEX, NPV and IRR. The two plotting fields retain the exact original Fig. 4 arrangement and contain fixed pairs 1 to 3 and pairs 4 to 6 in the prespecified pair order. Connector styles are retained from the original production asset for visual continuity and should not be interpreted as a new mutually exclusive classification.

Panel b shows central cumulative discounted cash flow over 25 years. Shaded regions are the 2.5th to 97.5th percentile summaries obtained by propagating the documented city-level input uncertainties. They are not independent confidence intervals and do not represent observed project distributions.

Panel c shows the all-building standardized baseline inputs and outputs: blended value of solar, LCOE, retail electricity rate and export rate. `Fig_6c.csv` reports the propagated median and the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles for every plotted component and city.

The standardized model does not resolve sector-specific load profiles, demand charges, battery operation, financing conditions or building-specific project returns. These limitations are stated in the caption, Methods and Supplementary Information.
