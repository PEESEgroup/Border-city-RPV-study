# Figure 3 Source Data notes

## Scope

Figure 3 contains the 12 primary cities in the six fixed border-city pairs. Detroit and Windsor are excluded from all four panels and remain a supplementary candidate-pair sensitivity case.

## Panel definitions

Panel a reports PV-positive-building prevalence, roof selection and conditional intensity. Prevalence is the number of PV-positive buildings divided by the total building count. Roof selection is the mean footprint area of PV-positive buildings divided by the mean footprint area of all buildings. Conditional intensity is mapped PV area divided by the complete footprint area of PV-positive buildings. Their product equals citywide PV utilization to numerical precision.

Panel b reports PV utilization and building-footprint area share in six footprint-area bins. The final bin is open ended at 1,000 m2 and above.

Panel c reports permutation-averaged contributions of prevalence, roof selection and conditional intensity to the signed all-building PV-utilization gap. All gaps use first-listed city minus second-listed city. Contributions sum exactly to the observed gap and are arithmetic allocations, not causal effects.

Panel d reports first-city-minus-second-city differences in PV-positive-building prevalence for six harmonized building-use classes. Hatching identifies low building count, city-level label-completeness risk or the heterogeneous others class. The panel does not contain policy-friction indicators.

## Quality checks

The maximum city-level factor-identity error is approximately 1.04e-15 percentage points. The maximum pairwise decomposition error is approximately 3.11e-15 percentage points. Panels c and d use the same six city-pair columns, separate vertical color scales and no Detroit or Windsor records.
